How it works

You're in one quadrant next to Ocellus. Purple portals on its two inner edges fire waves along their lanes. The orange portal on each edge doesn't fire, so the only safe spot is where the two orange lanes cross. Click to move there before the wave fires. Movement works like OSRS: one tick is 600 ms, running moves 2 tiles per tick, walking moves 1, diagonals come first, and clicks take effect on the next tick.

After a few waves, purple pools appear on the floor (you get hit for ending a tick on one), followed by an orange floor portal. Step on it before the barrage to teleport to a new quadrant.
